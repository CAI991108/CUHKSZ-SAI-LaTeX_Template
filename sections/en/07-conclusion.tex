\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This report presented and evaluated a human-in-the-loop document triage assistant. The
prototype puts the model proposal, the evidence spans, the human decision and the audit trail
into one workflow. The small pilot shows a shorter review time and more complete evidence,
while keeping the failure cases inspectable. The contribution is not the classifier itself
but the workflow that keeps the human decision and its supporting evidence together.

Future work has three directions: a larger corpus with more topics; better evidence
extraction so that low-confidence items also receive a readable explanation; and a
longitudinal study in a real deployment to see how reviewer trust and independent judgement
change over time.
